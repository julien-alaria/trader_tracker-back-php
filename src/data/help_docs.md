# Registration (user)

Go to the registration page and fill out the form with your name, email, and a password (at least 6 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit, and a special character). A standard user account is created automatically.

# Registration (analyst)

From the analyst registration form, select a specialization: Forex, Nasdaq, or Comex. Your account is created with "pending validation" status—an administrator must approve your profile before you can publish recommendations.

# Log in

Enter your email and password on the login page. Upon successful login, you are redirected to your profile page, which varies depending on your role (user, analyst, or administrator).

# Track an asset

From the detail page for an asset (stock, currency, or commodity), click the "Track Asset" button to add it to your personal watchlist, which is visible from your profile.

# Stop following an asset

From your watchlist, click "Unfollow" next to the relevant asset to remove it from your list.

# Follow an analyst

From an analyst's profile page, click "Follow" to receive their recommendations in your follow feed.

# Publish a recommendation (analyst)

If your analyst account is validated, you can publish a recommendation (Buy, Sell, or Hold) for an asset within your area of ​​specialization, accompanied by a comment of up to 280 characters.

# Edit profile

From your profile page, the "Update Your Profile" form allows you to change your name, email, password, and profile picture.

# Delete your account

In the "Danger Zone" section of your profile, the "Delete my account" button permanently deletes your account, as well as your watchlist and subscriptions, upon confirmation.

# Administrator role

An administrator can view the list of all users, approve pending analyst accounts, and modify or delete any account or recommendation.
